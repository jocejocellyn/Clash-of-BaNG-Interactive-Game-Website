The Spark In The Dark

Clash of BaNG began not as a guild, but as a conversation between three strategy enthusiasts in a small Jakarta cafe. Driven by a shared obsession with high-risk Dark Troop tactics, the community officially materialized in late 2018. What started as a local Discord server quickly evolved into a sanctum for those who believe that victory is only sweet when won through complex, legendary maneuvers.

"We didn't want to just play the game, we wanted to master the darkness within it."
